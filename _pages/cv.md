I work on semiconductor photonics, especially PCSEL device modeling, numerical verification, and automated design workflows.

## Research Experience

**PCSEL device research and computational modeling** · CUHK-Shenzhen · August 2025–present

I joined the group as a research assistant in August 2025 and began my doctoral studies in September 2026.

- Build GaAs PCSEL models in COMSOL and Lumerical FDTD; analyze band structures, cavity modes, and geometry and epitaxial parameter sweeps.
- Develop Python coupled-wave calculations and examine mode identity, numerical convergence, and agreement with independent implementations. Organize COMSOL–CWT batch results with consistent parameters, units, diagnostics, and source indexes.
- Connect solvers through Python/Java APIs and record simulation-to-report workflows. Study reinforcement learning and reliability-aware Bayesian optimization, described in the two preprints below.
- Participate in photoluminescence, optical/electrical pumping, L-I-V, spectral and beam characterization, ICP process tuning, KLayout mask work, and SEM-based process analysis.

**LN/LT waveguide mode analysis and phase matching** · Shandong University · January 2024–June 2025

Advised by Prof. Lei Wang. Built COMSOL models for thin-film lithium niobate and lithium tantalate ridge waveguides; studied geometry-dependent effective indices and phase-matching wavelengths.

**Memristor-based reservoir computing** · Shandong University · October 2022–October 2023

Advised by Prof. Limei Zheng. Processed text and speech datasets, implemented MATLAB training workflows, and debugged code for multimodal recognition and spatio-temporal learning.

**Black-hole accretion simulation** · Shanghai Astronomical Observatory, CAS · Summer 2022

Advised by Prof. Feng Yuan. Used the ZEUS hydrodynamics code to study gas accretion.
